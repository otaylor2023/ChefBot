
HANDHELD TOOLS AND POURING
- Utensils are used while held: pick the spoon or spatula up, align to the pan or bowl, then
  lower_into(container) and stir(seconds). Stirring in a bowl mixes; stirring food in a pan
  while it cooks tosses it (scrambled eggs, a stir-fry); food left unstirred cooks unevenly.
  Stir in turns of 20-30 s and check how it is going. lift_out() when done, then put the
  utensil down.
- pour(container) tips what the held bowl or pan contains into another container or onto
  a plate. Align to the target first.
