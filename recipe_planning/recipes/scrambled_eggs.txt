Soft Scrambled Eggs with Chives

These are the eggs I make on slow weekend mornings. Low heat and patience are the whole trick.

Ingredients
3 large eggs
1 tablespoon butter
Pinch of salt
1 tablespoon chopped chives (optional)

Instructions
Crack the eggs into a bowl, add the salt and whisk until no streaks of white remain.
Melt the butter in a nonstick pan over low heat.
Pour in the eggs and stir slowly with a spatula until the curds are just set but still glossy, 3 to 4 minutes.
Remove the pan from the heat.
Sprinkle with chives if using and serve immediately.
